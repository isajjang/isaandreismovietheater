How to duel

Draw 6 cards on turn one and 2 on subsequent turns. Unplayed cards stay in your hand. Maximum hand size: 7. If you exceed 7, click cards to discard until you have 7; then you can end your turn. Cards cost energy. Weak increases incoming damage by 40% per stack (1 stack ×1.4, 2 ×1.8). Poison deals damage each enemy turn and decays by 2. Combo (Melee): Quick Cut builds 1 Combo and gains +2 damage per existing stack. Heavy Blade spends every Combo for +4 damage per stack. Combo resets next turn.
Weak: Each stack makes its target take 40% more damage.

Click a card to play it. Attack cards damage the enemy, skills grant block or healing, powers strengthen future attacks. End your turn to let the enemy act. Survive and reduce the enemy to 0 HP.
